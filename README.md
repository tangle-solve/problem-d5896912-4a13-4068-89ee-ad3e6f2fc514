### CHEM-000401: Site-resolved explanation of flerovium’s two adsorption populations
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Quantify contributions from weak terrace adsorption, surface diffusion, and trapping at rare active gold sites to flerovium deposition. Use characterized surfaces and independently testable kinetic predictions.
- **Status:** A 2022 study reconciled contrasting adsorption observations using inhomogeneous surfaces and a proposed two-stage mechanism. Experimental discrimination of the individual adsorption states remains the narrower unresolved target.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Separates intrinsic element reactivity from the microscopic structure of the detector.
